Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RED REISHI LLC ("we", "us") handles information when you use the PICO mobile app and related services ("PICO"). PICO sells travel eSIM data plans. We built PICO to collect as little as possible: there are no PICO accounts, no advertising, no tracking and no location collection.

1. Information we collect

  • Order information — the destination and data plan you choose, price, currency, order number, order status and timestamps.
  • eSIM details — the eSIM activation (installation) code and identifiers issued for your order, so you can install and manage the plan.
  • Payment references — an identifier for your payment from our payment processor. We never receive or store your full card number. Card details are entered into and processed by Stripe.
  • Referral information — if you use or share a referral code, the code and the credit it generated.
  • Support messages — if you email us, your email address and anything you include in your message.
  • Technical data — like every internet service, our servers and hosting provider receive your IP address and basic request information (time, app version, error details) in short-lived security logs.

PICO does not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, advertising ID, or browsing activity. PICO does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. Information stored on your device

Your order numbers and plan details are saved on your phone so the My Plans tab can show them without an account. Deleting the app removes this information from your device.

3. How we use information

  • To process your purchase and deliver your eSIM.
  • To show your plans, data allowance and expiry dates.
  • To answer support requests, process refunds and apply referral credits.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, keep the service secure, and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • Stripe — payment processing (stripe.com/privacy).
  • eSIM network partners — the wholesale provider that issues your eSIM receives the order reference and plan needed to activate it. Your mobile data traffic is carried by local mobile networks under their own policies.
  • Hosting provider — our servers run on Render (render.com/privacy) in the United States.
  • Authorities — when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for as long as needed to provide your plan and for up to 7 years where tax and accounting rules require it. Security logs are kept for up to 30 days. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete information about your orders by emailing redreishilife+pico@gmail.com with your order number. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights, including the right to object to processing and to complain to your local data protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. We respond within 30 days.

Legal bases (EU/UK): performance of a contract (delivering your plan), legal obligation (tax records), and legitimate interests (security and fraud prevention).

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Payment data is handled by Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 provider. Access to our systems is limited and protected. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your device protected.

8. International transfers

PICO is operated from the United States. If you use PICO from another country, your information is transferred to and processed in the United States with appropriate safeguards.

9. Children

PICO is not directed to children under 13, and purchases require the account holder of the payment method. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
