# PICO Acceptable Use Policy (DRAFT — requires legal review)

**This is a working draft structure, not legal advice or final legal text.**

You may not use PICO services for:

- Fraud, identity theft, or impersonation of any person or company
- Phishing, spam, or unsolicited bulk messaging
- Robocalling or telemarketing where prohibited
- Harassment, threats, or extortion
- Money laundering, sanctions violations, or terrorist financing
- Illegal gambling or malware distribution
- Account takeover of any service
- Caller-ID spoofing or presenting a number you are not authorized to use
- Number abuse, rapid number acquisition for evasion, or any attempt to
  circumvent telecommunications regulations or carrier controls
- Any activity prohibited by applicable law or by our providers' contracts

## Enforcement

- Violations may result in immediate suspension of the account, numbers, eSIM
  provisioning, purchases, or transactions — suspension does not require an
  app update.
- Suspicious activity is classified LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / BLOCKED for
  operational response; these are operational controls, not legal judgments.
- Customers must affirm acceptance of this policy before using regulated
  communications services.
